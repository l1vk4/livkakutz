ÓRBITA

Overview:

Órbita is an interactive museographic installation that translates how music recommendation systems work into an embodied, immersive experience. Developed as a thesis project, it integrates user research and an Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI) framework, and was validated through three cycles of prototyping and testing with real users.

Specifications:

  • User research and Inverted Double Diamond methodology
  • Embodied interaction design and immersive experience
  • Iterative prototyping and testing
  • Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI) framework
  • Museographic design and installation setup
  • Visual system and graphic identity
  • Built using HTML, JSON and JS programming, and through TouchDesigner
