NOKIA 2660 FLIP PHONE

Overview:

3D modeling project of the Nokia 2660 Flip Phone. This work explores industrial design through the precise digital recreation of the device, paying close attention to the details and proportions of the original model.

Specifications:

  • 3D modeling in Rhino
  • Rendering in KeyShot
  • Proportion studies
  • Technical views
  • Product presentation
